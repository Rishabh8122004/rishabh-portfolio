I am Rishabh, a Computer Science undergraduate at MLVTEC, an RTU-affiliated engineering college in Bhilwara, Rajasthan. During the early phase of my college journey, I was introduced to C programming. Learning pattern printing became a turning point for me, as I began creating patterns using logic and mathematics. For the first time, programming started to make sense to me, and I developed a genuine interest in Computer Science. I then started learning Data Structures and Algorithms in C++ and have solved over 800 problems on Leetcode (My LeetCode Profile) over the span of two years.

Currently, I am in my final year, where I am trying to balance my curiosity for Computer Science with the responsibilities that come with building a career in the field. My curiosity lies in understanding how computers work at a fundamental level. I find subjects such as Data Structures, Computer Architecture, Digital Logic, Compilers, and Operating Systems particularly fascinating, and I continuously strive to deepen my understanding of these concepts.

At the same time, I believe that understanding the fundamentals is only one part of becoming a good Computer Science professional. Learning practical technologies, exploring areas such as Web Development, and gaining industry experience are responsibilities I have taken upon myself to prepare for the professional world. As part of this journey, I am currently pursuing an internship at InternNova, where I am gaining practical experience while continuing to develop my technical skills.

Apart from my academic and professional pursuits, I have a keen interest in exploring stories that inspire, offer new perspectives, and teach me something meaningful.

I also enjoy staying active through fitness activities and value spending quality time with my friends and family.

I have a strong curiosity about technology, animal behaviour, different places, and the diverse cultures around the world. I enjoy learning about them and understanding the world from different perspectives.

As I move forward, I hope to keep strengthening my fundamentals, building meaningful projects, and gaining experiences that allow me to turn my curiosity into practical knowledge.
